How to Play

Welcome to Cyberdash! This game will teach you all about cybersafety, and how to be aware online. To play the game you must collect all the safe points by moving using your arrow keys. Make sure to not collect the fake links. You get 10 points for every safe key you collect! You have three lives, however by collecting the stars you can reset your lives to three.
