Origins

HYPERNOVA Studios was founded in 2026 by a team of three high school students, with a passion for game development and a dream of spreading enjoyment all across the world through fun-to-play video games.

Mission

The video game space has historically been dominated by large corporations, such as Ubisoft, EA, Bethesda, and others. Very recently have independent developer studios been able to complete next to these multi billion dollar corporations, such as that of Sandfall Interactive and Team Cherry

At HYPERNOVA Studios, We wanted to contribute to this ever growing movement of cheap, accessible, financially unrestricted video games that people can actually play, actually afford and actually enjoy.
